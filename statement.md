# Problem Statement

Students living away from home lose track of where their monthly allowance goes,
and have no simple way to see category-wise spending or get warned before
overspending.

# Solution

A command-line Expense Tracker & Budget Analyzer written in Python.

* **Module 1, Transactions** (`transactions.py`): add, view, edit and delete
  expenses with category, amount and date.
* **Module 2, Budget manager** (`budget.py`): set monthly limits per category
  and get warnings at 80% and 100% of the limit.
* **Module 3, Analytics** (`analytics.py`): category totals, monthly summary,
  highest spending category, average daily spend, top 3 expenses and CSV export.
